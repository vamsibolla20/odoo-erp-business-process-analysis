# Business Case

WA Furniture is a fictional Perth-based make-to-order furniture manufacturer. The case describes heavy spreadsheet use, an ageing MRP system, a standalone accounting package and paper-based information exchange.

## Key Problems
- Errors and misplaced information as spreadsheet use grew
- Delays in payment and invoice processing due to missing documentation
- Difficulty accurately quoting custom orders
- Time-consuming supplier quotation comparison
- Limited production cost/time information
- Duplicate and obsolete BOMs
- Inventory replenishment reliant on employee judgement

The project evaluated Odoo as an integrated ERP covering Accounting, Procurement, Sales, Production and Inventory Management.
