# Odoo Configuration Summary

## Sales
Configured product variants and units of measure, a custom-product structure, a VIP pricelist for the 5% loyal-customer discount, delivery methods and 30% down-payment invoicing.

## Procurement
Used RFQs and alternatives for supplier comparison, preferred-supplier purchasing and purchase approval with a $1,000 threshold.

## Manufacturing
Enabled make-to-order/replenish-on-order, configured variant-based BOMs and an editable custom-product BOM template, and used manufacturing reporting for cost/time tracking.

## Inventory
Configured reordering rules for automated replenishment.

## Accounting
Configured customer invoicing, down payments and payment registration.

## Intended Business Value
The configuration demonstrates improved process integration, consistency, purchasing control, BOM management, inventory replenishment and invoice/payment processing. The project documents do not establish a measured percentage improvement.
